# 1.3 Our application after Kubernetes

**The engineering team overcame their initial hesitations, completing the migration rapidly and unlocking major architectural, operational, and financial advantages.**

**☁️ Multi-Cloud & Deployment Infrastructure**

- **Primary Cloud:** The main explorecalifornia.com site runs smoothly as a Kubernetes deployment within **AWS Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS)**.

- **Multi-Cloud Scaling:** During peak seasons, they can easily spin up additional website replicas on **Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)** to handle traffic cost-effectively.

- **Package Management:** The application is deployed using **Helm**, allowing the product team to quickly tweak website behavior and easily run user experiments.

**💻 Improved Developer Experience**

- **Local Environments:** Fights over dedicated development environments have ended; engineers can now spin up smaller, local versions of the entire website stack directly on their laptops.

- **Isolated Dependencies:** Because previously unstable downstream services (like databases or queues) are now containerized, developers can seamlessly recreate lightweight versions of them whenever needed.

**📈 Business Impact**

- **Release Velocity:** Explore California can now deploy feature updates as frequently as they want.

- **Competitive Edge:** This transition allows them to match or exceed the shipping speed of rivals like "Explore Texas."
